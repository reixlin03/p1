\section{Overview}

This report identifies TPU IDs that contain MTR stations that are colored on the map (Purple, Yellow, or Blue), excluding Quarry Bay and North Point stations.

\subsection{Colored Station Categories}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Purple (Tseung Kwan O Line):} Po Lam, Hang Hau, LOHAS Park, Tseung Kwan O, Tiu Keng Leng, Yau Tong
    \item \textbf{Yellow (West Rail):} Tuen Mun, Siu Hong, Tin Shui Wai, Long Ping, Yuen Long, Kam Sheung Road, Tsuen Wan West, Mei Foo, Nam Cheong
    \item \textbf{Blue (Ma On Shan Line):} Wu Kai Sha, Ma On Shan, Heng On, Tai Shui Hang, Shek Mun, City One, Sha Tin Wai, Che Kung Temple, Tai Wai
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Total Colored Stations (excluding Quarry Bay and North Point):} 24 stations
